The low-stock report lists the variants whose stock fell below their reorder point. It is your shopping list for the day.

## What it needs to work

Real stock levels. Because a stock level is created by the first movement, a variant with no movements never appears in the report — even if it is published and sold out in practice.

  src={bajoStock}

## How to use it

1. Check the report before placing orders, not after.
2. For each variant, confirm whether the stock left as a sale or as shrinkage: it changes the urgency.
3. If something is always on the list, its reorder point is probably mis-calibrated.

## Low stock vs. the public label

They are different things. The report looks at your real inventory; the label customers see ("out of stock", "low stock") is a manual field on the variant. When the report flags something, update the label too if there is nothing left.
