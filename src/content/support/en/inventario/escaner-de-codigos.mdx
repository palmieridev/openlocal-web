The scanner uses the device camera to find a variant by its barcode or SKU, without hunting through the list.

## Where it lives

The scan button sits inside **Inventory** and **Products**, where scanning a code leads to a concrete action. It is not in the sidebar, because scanning on its own is not a destination.

## Requirements

- Camera permission. Your browser asks the first time; if you decline, you have to re-enable it in the site settings.
- The variant must have its barcode or SKU saved.

## When the code is not found

You get the option to search manually. The usual causes are a variant without a saved code, or a printed code that belongs to the supplier's packaging rather than the one you entered.

<Callout tone="note" title="Light and focus">
Reading improves a lot with even light and the code about 15 cm from the camera. If the packaging is glossy, tilt the product to avoid the glare.
</Callout>
