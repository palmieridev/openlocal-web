Openlocal tracks inventory through movements: you do not edit a stock number, you record what happened and the system works out the balance.

## Recording a movement

In **Inventory → Log movement**, pick the variant, the type and the quantity.

Types cover stock in (purchase, return, positive adjustment) and stock out (sale, shrinkage, negative adjustment). Picking the right one matters: restocking analytics treats a sale differently from shrinkage.

<Callout tone="warning" title="The first movement creates the stock level">
A variant has no stock row until you record its first movement. That is why a freshly published product shows in the storefront but not under Stock levels.
</Callout>

## Stock levels and Movements

The **Stock levels** tab shows what you have right now, per variant. The **Movements** tab shows the history that explains it: what came in, what went out, when, and of which type.

When a number looks wrong, the answer is almost always in the history: a movement with the wrong type, or entered twice.

## Good habits

- Record a purchase the day the goods arrive, not the day you pay for them.
- Log shrinkage even when it stings: without it, restocking suggestions come out too low.
- After a physical count, correct with an adjustment rather than inventing a purchase.
