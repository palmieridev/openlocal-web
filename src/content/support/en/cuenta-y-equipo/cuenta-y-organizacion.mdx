## Account vs. organisation

Your account is personal: email, password and session. The **organisation** represents the business and is what owns the catalogue, the inventory and the storefront.

A shopper account needs no organisation. Registering a business creates the organisation and links your account to it.

## Changing language

Language is chosen on the site, not in your profile: the Spanish version lives at the plain address and English under `/en/`. Your choice is remembered for next time, but the address always wins.

## Signing out on a borrowed device

From your avatar, in the corner of the dashboard. Signing out does not affect your public storefront: it stays visible to customers.

## If the dashboard will not load

<Callout tone="note" title="Check in this order">
1. That your session is still open (sign in again if the avatar is missing).
2. That you are in the right business, if you have more than one.
3. Reload the page: the dashboard keeps the business reference in a cookie, and a stale session can end up without it.
</Callout>

If the dashboard still will not load after that, write to us with the exact address you were on.
