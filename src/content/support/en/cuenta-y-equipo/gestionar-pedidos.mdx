You need access to **Pickup orders** to follow this tour. Configure products and hours first. The tour does not create orders or change their status.

## 1. Find requests

Open **Pickup orders**, filter by date or status and select **Refresh**. Check usage and renewal. Requests arrive when customers save them in the storefront, independently of sending WhatsApp.

Use **New request** for phone or walk-in orders when available.

## 2. Review and confirm

Open an order and check products, quantities, time and notes. Confirm only when you can fulfil it; confirmation checks capacity and limits. You can also reject it.

A request counts when first confirmed. Cancelling it later does not restore quota. Pending, rejected or expired requests that were never confirmed do not count.

## 3. Record preparation

Move to **Preparing**, then **Ready** as work progresses. These statuses are recorded by your team; a customer's WhatsApp message does not change them automatically.

For goods sold by weight, record the final quantity from the details page. **Changing quantities does not automatically recalculate the total.** Calculate the amount and enter it separately in **Final total**, save your changes, then check quantities and total before notifying the customer or handing over the order.

## 4. Notify and hand over

**Open WhatsApp** prepares the updated message; you send it manually. Customers can also check status through their private link.

Mark the order **Collected** when handed over. If it is not collected, record the appropriate status. Use cancellation when cancelled rather than marking it delivered.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Collection does not record payment">
Openlocal does not charge the customer or deduct inventory when you mark Collected. Collect payment directly and record any inventory movements separately.
</Callout>

## 5. Keep the history

Orders and their history remain when intake is paused or the subscription ends. Access to screens and actions still depends on the current plan. Keep operational notes in the record and avoid sharing the customer's private link.
