Subscribe to **Appointments** in **Modules → Subscription** before starting. The tour requires sign-in and access to the module settings. It highlights controls without saving changes.

## 1. Create bookable services

Open **Appointments → Settings** and find **Services**. Under **New service**, enter the name, duration in minutes, description and optional price. Keep it **Active** and **Public**, then save that form.

For example, Wetdog could create “Full bath” with a 60-minute duration. Having “Full bath” in Products does not add it here automatically: the catalogue shows what you sell; appointment services define what can be booked and how long it takes.

## 2. Create a calendar

Under **Calendars**, create an active calendar for the person, station or space providing the service. Save its form separately. Essential includes the calendar allowed by that plan; Business supports multiple calendars and options such as service assignments and custom hours. Check current limits in Subscription.

For Wetdog, “Bathing station” could be one calendar. If two stations can work simultaneously, configure separate calendars when your plan allows it.

## 3. Set availability and rules

Under **Weekly availability**, uncheck **Closed** for working days and set opening and closing times. These hours use the business time zone.

Under **Appointment rules**, set:

- Minimum notice before a request.
- How many days ahead customers may request.
- How early customers must cancel.
- The interval between offered start times; this does not replace service duration.
- Exceptional closed dates.

Business adds preparation buffers per service and other advanced options. A 60-minute service still occupies 60 minutes when start times are offered every 30 minutes.

## 4. Enable intake and save

Check **Accept new requests** and select **Save settings**. This saves intake, hours and rules, not the separate service or calendar forms.

Open **Public storefront**. With current access, intake enabled, an active public service, an active calendar and availability, customers can request an appointment through the storefront button.

## 5. Try the complete flow

From the storefront, choose a service and time and save a request using fictional contact details. Check that it appears in Appointments and confirm it from its details page. Verify the status using the customer's private link. Cancel the test when finished; test confirmations also consume quota.

## If the button or times are missing

Check your subscription, intake, an active public service and an active calendar. Review open days, closed dates, notice and service duration: the service must fit in available hours. Check calendar-specific hours and service assignments if you use them.

<Callout tone="note" title="A request is not a confirmation">
Customers save pending requests. The business must confirm them, and availability is checked again then. WhatsApp is optional and messages must be sent manually.
</Callout>
