## Choose the module you need

In **Modules**, choose Appointments for services or Pickup orders for goods. Essential and Business apply to the selected module. The second module is optional and shares the tier; each module has its own confirmation allowance. Current prices and limits appear in **Subscription**.

Both tiers use prepared WhatsApp messages. You do not need to connect Meta, and messages are not sent automatically. Your subscription pays for Openlocal's tools; it does not charge your customers.

## Configure before accepting requests

For Appointments, create services with durations and a calendar, then define weekly availability and closed dates. Business adds multiple calendars, individual calendar schedules, and preparation buffers.

For Pickup orders, enable eligible variants and set minimum and maximum quantities and allowed increments. Set pickup windows and minimum notice. Business adds window capacity and daily cutoffs.

Enable **Accept new requests** when configuration is ready. You can pause intake from Modules; existing requests remain available.

## Handle each request

The queue shows requests received through the storefront. Use **New request** to enter a phone or walk-in request. Confirm or reject from the details page. Confirmation checks availability, capacity, and your plan allowance; if another operator took the last available place, the request is not confirmed.

After saving a change, **Open WhatsApp** prepares the updated message. You send it manually. Record completion, cancellation, or a no-show from the same page. Pickup orders do not automatically deduct inventory.

## Understand the allowance

A request consumes one confirmation the first time it is confirmed, including requests you enter yourself. Pending, rejected, or expired requests that were never confirmed do not count. Cancelling a confirmed request does not restore the allowance.

Upgrading keeps the current period's usage. You can continue fulfilling existing commitments after reaching the limit. The subscription page shows the renewal date.

## Changes and billing

Choose your tier and modules and review the change before confirming. The payment provider calculates applicable amounts. Access depends on confirmation from the provider; returning from a payment page alone does not activate it.

Use **Manage payments and invoices** to view invoices, update the payment method, or manage cancellation. When downgrading, review the retained calendar and effective date. Subscription changes do not delete your appointments, pickup orders, or their history.
