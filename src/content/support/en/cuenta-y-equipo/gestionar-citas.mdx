You need access to **Appointments** to follow this tour. Set up services, a calendar and availability before receiving requests. The tour does not create or confirm appointments.

## 1. Review the queue

Open **Appointments**. The counter shows confirmations used and your period limit. Filter by date, status or calendar, then select **Refresh**. You can switch between list and week views.

Storefront requests appear after customers save them, even if they do not send WhatsApp. For phone or walk-in requests, use **New request** when intake and your access allow it.

## 2. Confirm or reject

Open a request and review the customer, service, calendar, time and notes. Confirm only when you can fulfil it. Openlocal checks availability and quota again on confirmation: a pending request does not guarantee the time.

The first confirmation uses one unit of the period allowance. Rejecting or expiring a never-confirmed request does not count; cancelling after confirmation does not restore quota.

## 3. Contact the customer

**Open WhatsApp** prepares a message with the updated status. You must select **Send** in WhatsApp. There are no automatic notifications and Openlocal cannot tell whether a message was read.

Customers can check status through their private link. Confirmed appointments also offer a calendar download. Share the private link only with people who should have access.

## 4. Record the outcome

From the details page, mark completion, no-show or cancellation as appropriate. If the time must change, use the detail controls and verify the result before notifying the customer.

Completing an appointment does not charge the customer or record an inventory sale.

## 5. Consult history

Use date and status filters to find previous records. Pausing intake or ending a subscription does not delete them. Retained history does not mean the module remains enabled: screen access and available actions depend on the subscription.

<Callout tone="note" title="When you reach the limit">
You can continue handling existing commitments. For new confirmations, check renewal or plan options in Subscription.
</Callout>
