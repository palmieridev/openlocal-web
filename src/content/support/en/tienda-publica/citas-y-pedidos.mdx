## Save your request

When a business enables Appointments or Pickup orders, its storefront shows a request button. Choose a service and time, or products, quantities, and a pickup window. Enter your name, phone including country code, and any notes the business needs.

Saving creates a request with a reference. The business can already see it in its dashboard. A pending request still needs confirmation: choosing a time does not guarantee that it is reserved.

## WhatsApp is optional

After saving, you can open a prepared message with the reference and summary. WhatsApp requires you to press **Send**. Openlocal cannot tell whether you sent the message or whether it was read. Closing WhatsApp or leaving the message unsent does not remove your saved request.

## Check or cancel

Open **View my request** and save the private link. You can copy it from that page; it contains an access credential, so share it only with people you trust. The link lets you check the status and cancel within the business's cancellation rules. Confirmed appointments offer a calendar download.

The page refreshes while visible. You can also press **Refresh**. WhatsApp notifications are not automatic. To change the time, contact the business: its team can update the existing request.

Expired pending requests cannot be confirmed. Create another request if you still need it. Expiration does not depend on sending the WhatsApp message.

## Quantities and payments

For products sold by weight, the amount is an estimate. The business can record the final quantity and total, which appear on your private status page. You pay the business directly; marking an order collected does not record a payment in Openlocal.
