## Opening hours

Each day of the week is either closed or has an opening and a closing time. Hours are stored in **your business's timezone**, so a customer visiting from another city sees your local hours, not theirs.

Shifts that cross midnight are valid: 22:00 to 02:00 shows correctly as open late.

<Callout tone="note" title="No hours does not mean closed">
Until you set opening hours, your storefront shows no open/closed state at all. Empty means *unknown*, not closed.
</Callout>

## Delivery area

The service area defines how far you go. It does two things: it lets the marketplace show your business to people inside your area, and it tells a customer whether you can serve them before they message you.

## Cover photo and logo

The cover is uploaded from **Settings** (JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 5 MB) and is the large image on your storefront. The logo is your organisation's image and is managed from your account instead.

## WhatsApp

There is no cart and no online payment: the contact button opens WhatsApp with your number. Check it whenever you change lines, because it is the only direct channel between your storefront and the customer.
