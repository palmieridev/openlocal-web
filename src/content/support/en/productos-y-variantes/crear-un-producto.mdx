In Openlocal a product is the item ("Soy candle") and a variant is the specific version you sell (200 g, lavender, $180). Price and stock live on the variant, not on the product.

## 1. Create the product

In **Products → New product**, fill in name, type and description. The name generates the slug that identifies the product in your storefront.

  src={productoNuevo}

/>

## 2. Add at least one variant

Without a variant the product cannot appear in the storefront, even when marked public. On the variant you set:

- **Price** in pesos, plus an optional short note next to it (for example "per piece" or "minimum 3").
- **SKU and barcode**, if you use them; these are what the scanner reads.
- **Public stock status**: the label customers see (available, low stock, out of stock, made to order).

<Callout tone="warning" title="The label is not computed">
Public stock status is a **manual** field on the variant, not a reflection of your inventory. Recording a sale does not change the label on its own.
</Callout>

## 3. Publish it

Mark the product as public and save. The combination that makes it visible is: public product **+** at least one variant.

## 4. Check the "Public" column

In the product list, that column tells you at a glance what is visible in the storefront. The filters above separate drafts from published items, and the search box filters by name, brand or slug as you type.
