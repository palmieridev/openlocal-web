In Openlocal a product is the item ("Soy candle") and a variant is the specific version you sell (200 g, lavender, $180). Price and stock live on the variant, not on the product.

## 1. Create the product

In **Products → New product**, fill in name, type and description. The name is what builds the address the product is published under in your storefront.

## 2. Add at least one variant

Without a variant the product cannot appear in the storefront, even when marked public. On the variant you set:

- **Price** in pesos, plus an optional short note next to it (for example "per piece" or "minimum 3").
- **SKU and barcode**, if you use them; these are what the scanner reads.
- **Public stock status**: the label customers see (available, low stock, out of stock, made to order).
- **Visible in the shop**: the eye switch, on by default. Turn it off to pull just that card.

<Callout tone="warning" title="The label is not computed">
Public stock status is a **manual** field on the variant, not a reflection of your inventory. Recording a sale does not change the label on its own.
</Callout>

## 3. Publish it

Mark the product as public and save. The combination that makes it visible is: public product **+** at least one variant that is visible too.

There are **two switches**: the product's one governs all of its variants, and each variant's own switch decides its card.

```text
shown in the storefront = product visible AND variant visible
```

That way you can retire a single colour without deleting the variant or hiding the whole product. Hide the last visible variant and the product drops out of your storefront and of marketplace search — empty listings are never published.

<Callout tone="note" title="Hidden is not out of stock">
Hiding a variant removes it from the storefront. Marking it out of stock leaves it visible with that label. **Public stock status** and **visibility** are separate things.
</Callout>

## 4. Check the "Public" column

In the product list, that column counts the variants your storefront shows: "3 of 4" means three published cards out of four variants. Above it, the **Variant cards in the shop** tile adds up those cards across your whole catalogue — cards, not products.

The filters use the same rule: **In the shop** lists products with at least one visible card, **Unpublished** the ones showing none today (hidden product, no variants, or every variant hidden). The search box filters by name or brand as you type.

## Photos

The photo belongs to the **variant**, not the product: each version shows its own colour or presentation, and that is the image on the card in your storefront. Upload it from the variant editor (JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 5 MB).

The **business** cover is separate and is uploaded from Settings.
