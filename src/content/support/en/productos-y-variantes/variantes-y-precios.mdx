## When to create a variant

Create a variant when **the price or the stock changes**: sizes, presentations, colours with their own stock. If only the photo or the description differs, it is still the same item.

A catalogue with too many variants becomes hard to count. If two versions always sell together and share a price, they are probably one.

## Prices

Prices are stored and shown in **Mexican pesos**, including on the English version of the site. You enter them with decimals (`180.00`) and the storefront formats them for the reader's language.

## The note next to the price

The short note appears beside the price in your storefront. Use it for conditions that change what the number means:

- "per kilo"
- "minimum 3 pieces"
- "shipping not included"

## Variant description

This is separate from the product description: use it for detail that only applies to that version (exact measurements, ingredients, lead times).
