The dashboard has five destinations, and each answers a different question. If you would rather see it live, the guided tour walks you through all of them.

## Home

Your day at a glance: sales, published products and stock alerts. It is computed from the movements you record, so it starts empty and fills up once you log the first one.

## Products

The catalogue. This is where you create products and their variants (size, colour, presentation). A product **needs at least one variant** before it can appear in your storefront.

## Inventory

Stock levels and movements. Every stock in and out is recorded here, and a variant's first movement is what creates its stock level.

## Analytics

Low stock tells you what is running out; EOQ works out how much to order each time so you do not tie up cash in inventory.

## Storefront

Opens what your customers see, with your public products, opening hours and WhatsApp number. It is the best place to check how a change landed before you promote it.
