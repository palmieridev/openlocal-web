Registering your business creates your Openlocal organisation and unlocks the dashboard: catalogue, inventory and public storefront. It takes a few minutes, and everything stays editable afterwards.

<Callout tone="note" title="Before you start">
- Have your address and opening hours to hand.
- An active WhatsApp number: it is how customers reach you.
- A cover photo in JPG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, up to 5 MB. Optional — you can upload it later.
</Callout>

## 1. Open the business sign-up

Pick **Sell** from the top menu, or go straight to `/register/business`. If you already have a shopper account you can keep the same session: only business accounts go through registration.

## 2. Fill in the business details

Name, category, short description and service area. The name defines your storefront address — `openlocal.store/business/your-business` — so check it before saving.

The description is the first thing a customer reads in the marketplace. One concrete line ("hand-poured soy candles in Coyoacán") works better than a generic paragraph.

## 3. Set opening hours and contact

Mark the closed days and save your WhatsApp number. Hours are shown in your storefront using the **business's own timezone**, not the visitor's, and shifts crossing midnight are supported (22:00 to 02:00, for example).

Until you set hours, your storefront does not claim to be closed: it simply shows no hours yet.

## 4. Publish your storefront

Review the preview and hit **Publish**. Your storefront appears in the marketplace and on the map straight away.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Publishing is not the same as having stock">
A product shows in your storefront when it is marked public **and** has at least one variant. Stock tracking is a separate subsystem: it starts when you record your first inventory movement.
</Callout>

## What's next

With the business registered, the natural next step is getting to know the dashboard and adding your first product.
